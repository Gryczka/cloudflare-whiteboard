# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

Harassment, sexualized conduct, trolling, personal attacks, and publication of others' private information are unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project. Examples include repositories, issue trackers, review threads, community spaces, and project events.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that violate this policy. Enforcement decisions should be fair, proportionate, and respectful of reporter privacy.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns through GitHub Issues without publishing private information. Maintainers may issue a private correction, warning, temporary restriction, or permanent ban depending on impact and repeated behavior.

All complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly. Maintainers will respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
